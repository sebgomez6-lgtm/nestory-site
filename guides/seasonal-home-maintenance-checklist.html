Seasonal Home Maintenance Checklist (Spring, Summer, Fall and Winter)

A little upkeep each season prevents most of the expensive surprises. Here's what to do and when.

Updated November 2026 · By the Nestory team

Short answer: every month, test smoke and CO detectors and clean the dishwasher filter. Every 1–3 months, change the HVAC filter. In spring, service the AC and clean the gutters. In summer, check outside caulk and the dryer vent. In fall, service the furnace and clean the gutters again. In winter, protect pipes and check for ice dams.

Every month

  • Test smoke and carbon monoxide detectors.
  • Clean the dishwasher filter.
  • Check the HVAC filter, and change it every 1–3 months (more often with pets or allergies).
  • Test GFCI outlets in the kitchen and bathrooms.
  • Run water in unused sinks and showers so drain traps don't dry out.

Spring

  • Have the air conditioner serviced before the first hot days.
  • Clean gutters and downspouts, and make sure water drains away from the foundation.
  • Inspect the roof for damaged or missing shingles after winter.
  • Test the sump pump if you have one.
  • Check outdoor faucets and hoses for leaks.
  • Review your home inventory before storm season.

Summer

  • Clean the dryer vent all the way to the outside.
  • Check caulk and weatherstripping around windows and doors.
  • Clean the refrigerator's condenser coils.
  • Keep plants and debris at least two feet away from the outdoor AC unit.
  • Look for signs of pests around the foundation.

Fall

  • Have the furnace or heat pump serviced before it gets cold.
  • Clean the gutters again after the leaves fall.
  • Drain and shut off outdoor faucets, and disconnect hoses.
  • Flush the water heater to clear sediment.
  • Have the chimney inspected and cleaned if you use a fireplace.
  • Replace smoke and CO detector batteries (or once a year, whenever you remember best).

Winter

  • Keep the house heated enough to protect pipes, and insulate any exposed pipes.
  • Know where your main water shut-off is in case a pipe bursts.
  • Watch for ice dams and icicles along the roof edge.
  • Check the attic for leaks or condensation.
  • Reverse ceiling fans to push warm air down.

Once a year

  • Replace the refrigerator water filter (about every 6 months for many models).
  • Check the water heater's pressure relief valve.
  • Test the garage door's auto-reverse.
  • Update your home inventory and warranty list.

Always follow your manufacturer's instructions, and call a professional for gas, electrical and roof work.
