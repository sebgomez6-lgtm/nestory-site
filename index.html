FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Nestory free?

Yes. Nestory is free to download, with no ads.

Is it available on Android?

Not yet. Nestory is available for iPhone today.

Is my information private?

Yes. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data or ask for your address. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app. Read the privacy policy.

Can I keep track of more than one home?

Yes. Keep separate inventories for your house, a rental or a vacation home, and switch between them in a tap.

What if I don't have the manual anymore?

Search for your model's manual online right from the app and save it, or add a PDF you've downloaded. If you still have the paper copy, scan it page by page.

How do I get help?

Visit the support page or email hello.nestory.app@gmail.com.
